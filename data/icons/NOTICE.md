  https://controllercons.github.io

They are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (see `OFL.txt`),
not under the GPL that covers Haptika. They are redistributed here as
individual SVGs for Haptika's symbolic icon set. Controllercons is an
unofficial icon set and is not affiliated with any console or controller
manufacturer.

The Haptika-specific icons (`haptika-*.svg`), `open-menu-symbolic.svg`, and the
application icons under `hicolor/` are original artwork for this project and
are covered by the project license (GPL-3.0-or-later).
